Privacy Policy

How the piBrick website may handle information when you visit or contact the project.

Last updated: 06 November 2026

Important: This is a website privacy notice. Purchases, preorders, payments, and account activity handled by external services are also subject to those services' privacy policies.

1. Scope

This policy applies to the piBrick public website and pages that link to it. It does not automatically apply to third-party stores, payment providers, community platforms, repositories, or other websites you may visit through links on this site.

2. Information that may be collected

Depending on the website hosting configuration and the features you use, the following information may be processed:

  • Technical and log information: IP address, browser type, device or operating-system information, requested pages, timestamps, and error information recorded by the hosting provider or security systems.
  • Information you choose to send: your email address and message contents if you contact the project by email or another communication channel.
  • Usage information: analytics or diagnostic information, if an analytics or measurement service is enabled on the website.

Do not send passwords, payment-card details, government identification numbers, or other sensitive information through general support messages.

3. How information may be used

Information may be used to operate, maintain, and secure the website; respond to questions and support requests; diagnose errors and improve performance; prevent abuse or attacks; and meet legal obligations where applicable.

4. Cookies and similar technologies

The website may use cookies or similar technologies if required by its hosting, security, embedded content, or optional analytics configuration. The exact technologies depend on the services enabled when you visit.

You can manage or block cookies through your browser settings. Some third-party features may not work as expected if cookies or similar technologies are blocked. Before publication, identify any analytics, advertising, consent-management, or other cookie technologies actually enabled on the site.

5. Third-party services and links

Some pages may link to or load content from third-party services, for example:

  • Video platforms: video content may be opened or embedded through YouTube or another video provider.
  • Stores and preorder services: product links may direct you to Tindie or a separate piBrick preorder website. Those services handle checkout and order information under their own policies.
  • Code hosting and community services: links may direct you to GitHub, Discord, or other project resources.

When you visit or interact with a third-party service, that provider may collect information under its own privacy policy. Review the provider's policy before using the service. We do not control the data practices of independent third parties.

6. Sharing of information

We do not sell personal information through this website. Information may be processed by website hosting, security, communication, or other service providers where necessary to operate the site or respond to you. Information may also be disclosed when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect the website, users, or project.

Order information submitted to an external store or preorder service is handled according to that service's policies and the seller's arrangements.

7. Data retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, including support follow-up, security, recordkeeping, or legal requirements. Hosting providers and third-party services may apply their own log and retention periods.

8. Security

Reasonable measures may be used to protect information handled through the website. However, no website, internet transmission, or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Your choices and rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, deletion of, or information about the processing of your personal information. You may also be able to object to or restrict certain processing.

To make a privacy request, use the contact details below. We may need to verify your request before acting on it. Some information may need to be retained where the law permits or requires it.

10. Children's privacy

This website is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through a channel controlled by the project, contact us so that we can review the request.

11. International visitors

Website hosting and third-party services may process information in countries other than the country where you live. Those countries may have different data-protection laws. Where required, applicable safeguards should be used for international transfers.

12. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the website or its services change. The current version will be posted on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.
